# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (22 Nov 2026)

First version, built on 21 Nov 2026 at the Agentic AI Hackathon by Product Space x Wispr Flow.

- Run page: Heard (marks on your own words, self-corrections struck through), Understood (owner, due date, quote as
  proof), Plan with approval cards for emails, reminders and board posts.
- Quote checker: an item whose quote is not in the update is rejected.
- Email delivery in the owner's space only; public visitors get a sandbox outbox.
- Team board, calendar feed (ICS), CSV and JSON export, weekly summary: approved tasks only.
- Memory of decisions and what replaced them; conflict cards.
- Eval of 24 fixed tasks with four measures (`docs/MEASURED.md`).
- English and Malay interface; Open Graph card and icons.
